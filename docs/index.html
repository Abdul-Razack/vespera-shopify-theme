Vespera Theme v2.0.0 — Official Merchant User Guide

Vespera Theme Documentation

Welcome to Vespera, an editorial Online Store 2.0 theme engineered exclusively for luxury skincare, botanical cosmetics, fragrance houses, and high-end wellness brands. Built with zero third-party framework overhead, Vespera achieves near-instant mobile load times, 100% WCAG AA contrast compliance, and full architectural alignment with official Shopify Theme Store standards.

Vespera Preset

Moody, nocturnal luxury palette (deep espresso, charcoal, and warm antique gold). Optimized for fine fragrance and botanical night elixirs.

Luminous Preset

Clean, airy minimalist editorial style (crisp white, soft sage, and deep forest green). Ideal for clinical dermatological skincare.

Botanical Preset

Warm organic parchment and terracotta tones. Handcrafted for organic herbalism, facial oils, and sustainable body rituals.

1. Installation & Initial Setup

To install Vespera into your Shopify store:

  1. From your Shopify Admin, navigate to Online Store → Themes.
  2. In the Theme library section, click Add theme → Upload zip file.
  3. Select vespera-v2.0.0.zip and click Upload file.
  4. Once processed, click Customize to launch the Shopify Theme Editor.

Shopify Best Practice: We recommend publishing the theme on an unpublished preview first while configuring your catalog and media settings.

2. Top Bar & Navigation Setup

The site header includes an interactive multi-slide Top Bar, sticky header behavior with scroll-up reveal, native predictive search, and multi-level mega navigation.

Configuring the Top Bar

  • In the Theme Editor, select Top bar under the Header Group.
  • Add multiple message slides (e.g. "Complimentary worldwide shipping over $75").
  • Enable automatic carousel rotation and adjust slide duration (3 to 10 seconds).
  • Select background and text contrast colors tailored to your active preset.

3. Product Page (PDP) & Reorderable Blocks

Vespera features 14 fully reorderable product blocks allowing merchants to craft editorial storytelling experiences:

  • Product Title & Vendor: Semantic heading levels with configurable font sizes.
  • Price & Unit Price: Automatically renders compare-at savings, tax notices, and unit price calculations (e.g. $12.00 / 100 ml).
  • Variant Picker: Choose between tactile visual option chips, dropdowns, and swatches. Multi-modal cues indicate sold-out and unavailable states.
  • Quick Buy & Dynamic Checkout: Branded primary CTA paired with native accelerated checkout (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay).
  • Editorial Tabs: Configurable accordion tabs for Bio-Active Ingredients, Application Ritual, and Clinical Studies.
  • In-Store Pickup Availability: Displays real-time location pickup options when retail inventory is present.

4. Slide-Out Ajax Cart Drawer

Vespera's Cart Drawer is built with framework-free Vanilla JS custom elements (<cart-drawer>):

  • Free Shipping Threshold Meter: Live progressive progress bar calculating remaining cart value required for free shipping.
  • Order Notes: Merchant-toggleable cart note field with debounced background auto-save to /cart/update.js.
  • Discount Applications: Real-time display of both automatic order-level promotions and line-item savings badges.
  • Accessibility Focus Trap: Complete keyboard trapping (Tab / Shift+Tab) with Escape key dismissal and return-of-focus.

5. Video & 3D Model Media Gallery

The product gallery supports interactive media rendering:

  • Still Photography: Pinch-to-zoom on mobile and high-resolution modal lightbox on desktop.
  • MP4 & WebM Video: Native responsive HTML5 video playback with looping controls.
  • 3D AR Models: Interactive GLB/USDZ model viewer with Shopify AR quick-look on iOS and Android devices.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between the Botanical, Luminous, and Vespera presets?

In the Shopify Theme Editor, click on the Theme settings (gear icon) in the top left, expand Presets, and select your desired aesthetic style. All typography, color tokens, and layout defaults will update instantaneously.

Does Vespera support multi-currency and multi-language?

Yes. Vespera natively supports Shopify Markets. The country and language selectors in the footer and mobile menu activate automatically when more than one market or language is enabled in Settings → Markets.

How do I enable Gift Card recipient forms?

When you create a Gift Card product in your Shopify Admin, Vespera automatically detects product.gift_card? and renders the native recipient form (recipient email, name, message, and scheduled delivery date) directly above the Add to Cart button.
